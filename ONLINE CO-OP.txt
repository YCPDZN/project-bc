SURVIVE THE UNDEAD - ONLINE CO-OP

This mode has a separate survival record. Campaign progress and character statuses are unaffected. It is unavailable in the Last Refusal menus.

1. Open SURVIVE THE UNDEAD, watch or skip the opening, and choose a character.
2. Choose MAPS > BC GOVERNMENT COMPLEX.
3. The host chooses HOST ONLINE CO-OP, then CREATE INVITE.
4. Send that code to one friend. They choose JOIN ONLINE CO-OP, paste the invitation and choose JOIN / CREATE REPLY.
5. The friend sends the reply code back. The host pastes it and chooses ACCEPT REPLY.
6. Repeat with separate invitations for up to three friends (four players total).
7. Guests choose READY. The host chooses START SURVIVAL.

The host runs enemies, waves, damage and revives. Leaving the host ends that session. Hold USE near a downed teammate to revive them. Service lifts connect the two floors. Supply crates restore health.

WINDOWS: WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, Shift run, R reload, E use lift / hold to revive, Esc pause.
TOUCH: left joystick moves; swipe the right side to aim; FIRE, RELOAD, RUN, USE and pause buttons appear in play.

Connections use WebRTC with direct invite/reply codes. There is no public matchmaking service or relay server included. Some restrictive networks may prevent a direct connection; an internet-wide deployment may require a TURN relay. Four-player connections and gameplay were tested in local browser sessions. Separate remote networks have not been verified.
