# Mobile controls

Touch controls appear automatically on touch devices and when a touchscreen is used.

- Left stick: move; in the car chase, accelerate, brake and steer.
- Swipe the right side: look and aim. Movement, aiming and firing support simultaneous touches.
- Hold FIRE: shoot. RELOAD reloads; weapon buttons switch weapons where available.
- Hold RUN: sprint. CROUCH is available in Chapter 1 and the Cabinet boss fight. DODGE appears in fights that support it.
- USE: interact with doors, keys, lifts, or train seats; HELP appears during the rescue.
- SLEEP: skip the train journey where the chapter permits it.
- JUMP: appears in Kevin's platform arena. BRAKE appears in the car chase.
- Tap MAP to open/close the Chapter 1 map, Whitehall battle map, or Peter's Piccadilly route map. TORCH toggles the flashlight.
- CAMERA switches first/third person in playable personal stories and Kevin's Uxbridge pursuit.
- FLY / LAND toggles flight in the Uxbridge pursuit; hold RISE / DOWN to change altitude. Aerial personal-story bosses also have RISE / DOWN controls.
- HEAL uses a medkit in personal-story fights where supplies are available.
- TAP!: one tap per QTE press; holding does not automatically complete QTEs.
- Pause button: open the existing pause menu. Free roam has EXIT instead. Cutscene and choice buttons remain directly tappable; Pindar has NEXT LINE for skipping spoken dialogue.
- 3D character viewer: swipe to rotate.

Every campaign route, Last Refusal variant, personal story, free roam, and Survive the Undead uses the shared touch controls. Buttons adapt to the current chapter and disappear during cutscenes and decisions. Portrait and landscape layouts include scrollable dialogue, touch-sized buttons and safe-area spacing. Landscape provides more room to see the world. Phone rendering uses a lower resolution cap while retaining the game's adaptive performance scaling. Recordings load when needed rather than preloading all chapter audio on phones.

Movement, aim and firing support multiple fingers. Cancelled touches, app suspension and orientation changes release held inputs. Controls do not require pointer lock. Keyboard and mouse remain available on Windows; clicking the mouse returns to desktop controls and its rendering resolution.

This adds browser/WKWebView input support, not a signed native iOS application. Validate on physical iPhone and iPad hardware when packaging the iOS app, including audio, performance, app suspension, and orientation changes.

In Before the Story / The Last Juko, CAMERA switches Josiah between first and third person, OPEN uses the nearby castle door, and FIRE/RELOAD operate his pistol. Room 25 takes over for a cinematic; touch controls return when another playable section begins.

Windows alternate fire: press or hold Z to shoot or use the current chain attack. Left-click and touch FIRE remain available. Releasing Z, pausing, leaving combat, or switching apps stops keyboard firing.
